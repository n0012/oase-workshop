# WO-007: Rat/Dog NCA & 70 kg Human Allometric Oral PK Projection (`OASE-PCSK9-014`)

## Key Findings
- **Preclinical Oral Bioavailability:** `OASE-PCSK9-014` demonstrates rat oral bioavailability of 54.0% {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.rat_oral_f_pct} and dog oral bioavailability of 61.0% {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.dog_oral_f_pct}.
- **70 kg Human Projection:** Two-species allometric scaling projects human oral bioavailability of 52.0% {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.projected_human_f_pct}, effective half-life (h) of 14.8 {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.projected_human_half_life_h}, and a QD oral tablet dose (mg) of 40.0 {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.projected_qd_oral_dose_mg} maintaining unbound hepatic trough coverage above `IC90`.
