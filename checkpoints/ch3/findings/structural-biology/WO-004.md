# WO-004-r1: PCSK9 3D Structure, AlphaFold 3 Co-Folding & Pocket Druggability (`IC-001` vs. `IC-002`)

## Summary Verdict
AlphaFold 3 co-folding and `fpocket` cavity profiling confirm a druggable inter-domain allosteric groove (`Pocket #1`) supporting `IC-001` over the flat EGF-A surface (`IC-002`).
