- **Relay: `docking.score_is_not_affinity`** — Report Vina score (`-9.4 kcal/mol`) as a predicted binding energy estimate, never as a measured biochemical `Ki` or `IC50`.
- **Relay: `docking.contact_is_not_binding_event`** — Geometric proximity in a static pose does not prove a thermodynamic hydrogen bond or salt bridge without experimental SPR/X-ray confirmation.
