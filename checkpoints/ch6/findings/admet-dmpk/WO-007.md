# WO-007-r1: Rat/Dog NCA & 70 kg Human Allometric Oral PK Projection (`OASE-PCSK9-014`)

## Summary Verdict
Preclinical rat/dog NCA and two-species allometric scaling support once-daily (QD) oral dosing in humans with an acceptable hERG safety margin (`> 30x`).

## Key Findings
- **Rat Oral Bioavailability (`NCA`):** Measured rat oral bioavailability (%) of 54.0% {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.rat_oral_f_pct}
- **Dog Oral Bioavailability (`NCA`):** Measured dog oral bioavailability (%) of 61.0% {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.dog_oral_f_pct}
- **Projected Human Oral Bioavailability:** Allometric 70 kg human oral `F` (%) of 52.0% {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.projected_human_f_pct}
- **Projected Human Half-Life:** Effective terminal half-life (h) of 14.8 {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.projected_human_half_life_h}
- **Projected Human QD Dose:** Once-daily oral tablet dose (mg) maintaining hepatic unbound `C_trough > IC90` of 40.0 {source: raw/pk/oase_pcsk9_014_scaled_pk.analysis.json $.metrics.projected_qd_oral_dose_mg}
- **Relay: `pk.allometric_scaling_is_projection`** — Allometric human PK parameters are two-species projections (`rat`/`dog`) and require clinical Phase 1 SAD/MAD verification.
